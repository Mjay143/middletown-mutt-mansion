Quick Answers

Common Questions

Where do the dogs sleep at night? +

Never in cages! Dogs roam our home freely and choose between sleeping upstairs with us or downstairs on our leather sectional and cozy dog beds.

Can we schedule a Meet & Greet first? +

Absolutely! We encourage local Middletown & Newport dog owners to stop by with their pup Monday–Friday between 8:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

What are drop-off hours and payment options? +

Drop-off and pick-up are by appointment from 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. We accept Cash and Venmo directly (or credit cards via Rover with a ~15% platform fee).

When do Holiday Rates apply in 2026? +

Holiday rates apply on major holiday weekends (MLK, Presidents’ Day, Easter, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Nov 24–28, Christmas/NYE Dec 23–Jan 2) plus the Newport Folk & Jazz Festivals.
